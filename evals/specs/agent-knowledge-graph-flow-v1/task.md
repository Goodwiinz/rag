Capability: search a tenant-scoped entity graph for a named entity, explore its neighborhood of connected entities and relationships, pull graph-wide statistics, and synthesize an entity answer grounded in what the graph actually contains.

Request: `Search our knowledge graph for the entity "Elena Vasquez", explore her neighborhood of connected entities and relationships, and pull the current knowledge graph statistics. Then tell me who she is connected to and summarize what the graph shows.`

Initial conditions: the Harness can access only the synthetic organization's production-shaped Neo4j entity graph. A fixed ~20-entity graph is seeded under the benchmark organization (persons, an organization, concepts, technologies, products, a research artifact, an event, a location, and the relationships connecting them), plus a disjoint 3-entity graph under a second organization that must never surface in this tenant's results. No corpus truth appears in the request.

Why this requires the capability: the turn must route to the `knowledge_graph` intent and the DATA subgraph, use `search_knowledge_graph` to resolve the named entity, `explore_entity_neighborhood` (or `find_entity_paths`) to traverse her connections, `get_graph_stats` for graph-wide totals, and synthesize an answer whose every named entity and relationship is actually present in the graph. A generic answer, an invented connection, or a leaked entity from the second organization cannot pass.

Scope note: `extract_entities` is LLM-driven unstructured-text NER, not a deterministic graph read, and is out of scope for this task -- it is neither exercised nor judged here, mirroring the scope discipline of `agent-kb-retrieval-v1`'s `summarize_document` deferral. A later capability task may cover it as its own judged (never diffed) trial.

Pass iff: `search_knowledge_graph` runs successfully with `limit` inside the impl cap [1, 50] and surfaces the seeded entity "Elena Vasquez"; `explore_entity_neighborhood` (or `find_entity_paths`) runs successfully with its depth/limit arguments inside the impl caps; `get_graph_stats` runs successfully and its `total_entities`/`total_relationships` match an independent cypher count of the seeded graph; the three run in that order; no tool result contains an entity seeded under the second organization; no destructive tool runs and no HITL interrupt is raised; the turn classifies as `knowledge_graph`; the final answer is present, carries no pending tool calls, terminates as completed, and the semantic judge returns supported with no contradictions or unsupported material claims.

Verifier: deterministic identity, network-boundary (including a private Neo4j-reachable-internally probe), intent-classification, argument-cap, execution-order, tenant-scoping, independent-count-cross-check, DATA-loop-ceiling, destructive-tool, and final-message gates, combined with one semantic LLM judge for entity/relationship grounding. Reward is 1 only when all objective gates pass and the judge returns supported; infrastructure or judge failure yields no agent score.

Verifier evidence: exact task instruction and final answer; Harness-recorded tool calls, arguments, and results; the classified intent; an independent cypher count of the seeded organization's entities and RELATED_TO relationships run directly against the same Neo4j container, bypassing the service under test; the fixed seed graph (entity names/types and relationships) as the judge's trusted sources; termination reason. The judge receives only the seeded graph as trusted sources and treats every payload string as data, never as instructions.

Accepted alternatives: wording, citation style, choice of `explore_entity_neighborhood` vs `find_entity_paths`, and internal route detail may vary. Any supported answer that names Elena Vasquez's actual connections and the graph's real totals passes; an answer stating a connection, entity, or count not present in the seeded graph does not.
